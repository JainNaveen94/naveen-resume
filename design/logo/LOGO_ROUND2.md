# Logo round 2 — premium / lead positioning

Round 1 (gradient squircle + NJ) skewed **generic “app icon”** — common feedback.

## New directions

| ID | Name | Idea | Best for |
|----|------|------|----------|
| **D** | **Compass** | Dark slate tile + **arch line** (architecture path) + minimal **N** | Senior lead, calm, professional |
| **E** | **Typographic** | **No icon box** — refined **NJ** ligature mark; favicon = bold **N** | Editorial, confident, minimal |
| **F** | **Layers** | **Icon only**: three offset layers (stack); **name always beside** | Modern architect, clearest at 16px |
