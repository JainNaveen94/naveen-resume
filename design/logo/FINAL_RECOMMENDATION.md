# Final logo recommendation

## Selected concept: **H — System graph**

### Why H (vs G, I, J)

| Concept | Principal / architect fit |
|---------|---------------------------|
| **H System graph** | **Best.** Reads as **distributed architecture** — one **lead hub** (principal) and **services** below. Familiar from diagrams reviewers know. |
| G Blueprint | Strong for “architect,” slightly busier at **16px** (corner ticks). |
| I Arch | Physical metaphor — weaker for **software** lead. |
| J Isometric | Good for “platform,” less about **orchestration / principal** role. |

### Tile style: **Dark slate** (`#0f172a`)

- **Consulting / principal** tone — calm, high-trust (not neon gradient tile).
- Pairs with your **light + dark** site; cyan **lead node** picks up any accent theme via surrounding UI.
- **Alternative:** `mark-transparent.svg` if you prefer no tile on a light glass header.
